      <h2>Introduction</h2>
      <p>
        I participated with a new project from MLDSpot Campaign (Cigarette Company) for their customer loyalty program. We have a team of three with UI/UX Designer and Back-end Developer, I'm the one in charge to develop the UI, make sure it is browser compatible, fast, and mobile responsive.
      </p>

      <h2>Project Overview</h2>
      <p>
        The MLDSpot Loyalty Program was designed to enhance customer engagement and retention for MLDSpot's cigarette products. The platform allows customers to:
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>Register and track their purchases</li>
        <li>Earn points for product purchases</li>
        <li>Redeem points for rewards and prizes</li>
        <li>View their transaction history</li>
        <li>Participate in special promotions and campaigns</li>
      </ul>

      <h2>My Role</h2>
      <p>As the Front-end Developer in a team of three, my responsibilities included:</p>

      <h3>UI Development</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Translating UI/UX designs into functional web interfaces</li>
        <li>Implementing responsive layouts that work across all devices</li>
        <li>Creating interactive components with jQuery</li>
      </ul>

      <h3>Performance Optimization</h3>
      <ul>

        <li>Implementing efficient JavaScript code</li>

      <h3>Cross-Browser Compatibility</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Ensuring consistent experience across different browsers</li>
        <li>Testing on Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge</li>
        <li>Implementing fallbacks for older browser versions</li>

      <h3>Mobile Responsiveness</h3>
      <ul>
        <li>Creating mobile-first responsive designs</li>
