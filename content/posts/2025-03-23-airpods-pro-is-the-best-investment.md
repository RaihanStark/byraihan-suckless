      <p>
        As a software engineer, the most important investments are courses, computers/laptops, multiple monitors, and noise-canceling headphones.
      </p>
      <p>
        I was thinking, <em>why would I buy a $249 earphone</em> when I could do the same thing with my $7 earphones? There’s no way I’m willing to buy that overpriced Apple product.
      </p>

      <h2>The Problem</h2>
      <p>
        I can’t work productively when there is too much noise around me. Because of this, I always listen to music as loud as possible so I can’t hear any noise with my earphones.
      </p>
      <p>
        It was great, but after a few years of doing that, my ears started ringing often, and it was painful.
      </p>
      <p>
        It turns out my ears were damaged, and I’m still 23 years old! I don’t know if I’ll still be able to hear at 50 if I continue this habit.
      </p>

      <h2>The Solution</h2>
      <p>
        I searched for noise-canceling headphones - they were supposed to block noise even if I didn’t play music. I didn’t believe it.
      </p>
      <p>
        I quickly bought the AirPods Pro 2, tried them for the first time, and I was <strong>MINDBLOWN</strong>!
      </p>
      <ul>
        <li>I can hear better with Transparency Mode since it already has a hearing-aid feature.</li>
        <li>I can work better in Active Noise Cancellation Mode. I don’t have to turn up the music loud - or even play any music - and still, there’s no noise!</li>
        <li>It protects my ears when I go outside and there’s a sudden loud noise; it automatically cancels it.</li>
      </ul>
      <p>
        Now I can’t leave home without them - not because I want to listen to music, but because I can hear <strong>better</strong> and they protect my ears.
      </p>

      <h2>Conclusion</h2>
      <p>
        This isn’t a review, just my experience trying noise-canceling stuff - and it was worth every penny.
      </p>
