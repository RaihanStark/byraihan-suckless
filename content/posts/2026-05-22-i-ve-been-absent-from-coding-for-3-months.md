<h2>The Absence</h2>
      <p>
        I've been absent from coding for 3 months straight due to a health issue. I felt like I was missing out on a lot of opportunities and I'm not sure how I'm going to get back on track.
      </p>
      <p>
        It is so frustrating that I'm not even able to work on anything, literally. My body just doesn't want to do what I want to do. I lost self-control.
      </p>

      <h2>Aftermath</h2>
      <p>
        I'm going to have to find a way to get back on track. I basically lost all of my motivation and skills. Three months is a long time to lose, especially in Software Engineering.
      </p>
      <p>
        You can miss out anything by the blink of an eye. Off one day, then a whole new framework came out. Now, imagine 3 months.
      </p>

      <h2>Conclusion</h2>
      <p>
        This doesn't mean I'm not going to try again. This is my whole career. Three months won't hurt me. I'm going to do it until I die.
      </p>
