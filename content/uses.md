Hardware, desktop environment, and tools I use daily.

## Hardware

- **Laptop**: Lenovo ThinkPad T490 (Intel Core i5-8365U, 24 GB RAM, 256 GB NVMe, 14" `1080p` IPS)
- **Audio**: Apple AirPods Pro 2

## OS & Desktop

- **OS**: Arch Linux
- **Window Manager**: `hyprland` - configured in Lua with a monochrome theme, zero gaps, no animations, and a solid black background
- **Login Manager**: Custom **sddm-suckless** monochrome theme
- **Status Bar**: `waybar` - minimal `dwm`-style bar showing battery wattage/voltage, AirPods status, and system load
- **Launcher & Utilities**: `fuzzel`, `dunst`, `wl-clipboard` + `cliphist`, `grim` + `slurp`, `imv`

## Terminal & CLI

- **Terminal**: `foot` (monochrome theme with dropdown and `btop` scratchpads) & `kitty`
- **Font**: `JetBrainsMono Nerd Font`
- **Shell**: `zsh` with `starship` prompt
- **CLI Tools**: `fzf`, `fd`, `zoxide`, `eza`, `bat`, `jq`, `btop`, `lazygit`, `airpods-tui`

## Development

- **Editors**: `nvim` (`lazy.nvim`) & `vim`
- **Languages**: Go, TypeScript/Node.js, Python
- **Browser**: `firefox`
